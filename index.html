Multiplayer

Everyone who clicks this lands in the same world — no codes. Build together, chat with T.

How to play / controls
  • WASD move · Space jump · Shift sneak/descend · Ctrl/hold W sprint
  • Mouse look · Left-click mine / attack · Right-click place block
  • 1–9 or scroll: select hotbar · E inventory & crafting · F toggle fly
  • T chat · Esc release mouse
  • Survive: watch health ❤ and hunger 🍗. Eat food, build shelter before night — zombies spawn in the dark!
